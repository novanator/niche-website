About

NICHE is the National Institute for Conservation, Health, and Ecology. NICHE advances interdisciplinary research to understand how ecological change connects the health of wildlife, ecosystems, and human communities.

Mission

The National Institute for Conservation, Health, and Ecology (NICHE) advances interdisciplinary research on the connections among biodiversity, ecological change, and health. We generate and apply scientific knowledge to conserve species, strengthen ecological resilience, and promote the health and flourishing of wildlife, ecosystems, and human communities.

Vision

We envision a world in which species and ecosystems thrive, and science guides decisions that sustain biodiversity, ecological resilience, and health across generations.

Research

Biodiversity conservation

Understanding species and ecosystems to inform conservation and support ecological resilience.

Wildlife disease ecology

Exploring how infections spread and persist in wildlife populations, and how ecological conditions shape disease dynamics.

Environmental change and health

Investigating how changes in the environment affect the interconnected health of wildlife, ecosystems, and human communities.
